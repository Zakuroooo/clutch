# 0003. No real player faces

**Date:** 13 Nov 2026 · **Status:** accepted

## Context
Player cards would look more familiar with portraits. We have no rights to photographs, and generating a real person's likeness without permission is a legal and ethical problem. It would also reflect badly on a company whose business depends on player and league relationships.

## Decision
Players are shown by name and as a jersey in team colours with their number. No photographs, drawings, silhouettes or generated likenesses. This is a rule for every contributor and every AI tool used on the project.

## Why
- No likeness rights are needed for a name used as a fact, or for a number.
- It became the visual identity: a jersey in team colours with the player's number.

## Cost
- Less instant recognition for a casual fan, who is exactly the user. The card's text has to do that work.
