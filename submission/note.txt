Clutch: the friend who tells you when to look up

Try it: https://clutch-one-mu.vercel.app and tap “Show me in 2 minutes”. No sign-up.

The fan problem.

Casual fans watch with the match beside their work. They look away, miss the one ball everyone talks about, and when the room roars they don’t know why. Live sport is long quiet stretches and a few seconds that matter, and nobody tells them which.

How Clutch helps.

One ring, the Pulse, fills before a ball that could change the match and calls you back. Moments you missed wait on a shelf with a drawn replay, explained in a world you already know: football, F1, chess, Bollywood or gaming. Code builds and checks every number. It replays a real IPL match as if live; there are no real users yet.

AI used.

A win-probability model (scikit-learn), trained on 3,137 past T20 matches, scores the stakes of every ball. On 673 held-out matches, its call-backs came before 52% of the biggest swings, against 22% for random ones. I built Clutch with Claude Code (Anthropic) for planning, code, tests and design review. The problem, product decisions and final review are mine.
