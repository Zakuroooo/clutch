# Data attribution

The files in this folder are adapted from ball-by-ball match data published by
**Cricsheet** (https://cricsheet.org) and are shared under the same licence, the
**Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0**
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

What was changed: one match was converted into `timeline.json` with a win chance and a
stakes value for every ball (computed by the model in `model/`), a synthetic replay time
for every ball, call-backs (`alerts.json`), moments, cards, and career counts for the
players in that match (`players.json`).

Not from Cricsheet, typed by hand: team colours and short codes in `teams.json`
(plain colours, no logos) and jersey numbers in `jerseys.json`.

Cricsheet's data is compiled by volunteers and is not an official record of any cricket board.
